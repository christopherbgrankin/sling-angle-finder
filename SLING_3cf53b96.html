Anchor angles

How to use: aim side-on to the rig, tap Freeze, then drag the points onto the ropes. Live mode also works: hold steady and line the rig up with the points. Hold the phone upright, side-on and square to the rig: an off-axis shot reads the angle wrong. Planning and training aid only. Loads are kg-force; check against doctrine and rated equipment (kN).
