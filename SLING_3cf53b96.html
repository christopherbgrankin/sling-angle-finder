Sling Angle

Live rig measurement

Uses the rear camera and the tilt sensor. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

Anchor angles

How to use: aim side-on to the rig, tap Freeze, then drag the points onto the ropes. Live mode also works: hold steady and line the rig up with the points. Keep the tilt chip near 0° pitch: an off-axis shot reads the angle wrong. Planning and training aid only. Loads are kg-force; check against doctrine and rated equipment (kN).
